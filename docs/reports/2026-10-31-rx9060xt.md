# AMD RX 9060 XT hardware evidence - 2026-10-31

Host: Garuda Linux, Linux 7.2.11-zen1-1-zen, Ryzen 5 7500F, Radeon RX 9060 XT (OpenCL gfx1200). Measurements were **user-provided terminal output**, not a simulated CI result.

## Phase A: read-only preflight

`v085/tools/reserved_vram_preflight.py --pool-mib 4096`:

- PCI `0000:03:00.0`, `vram_total_mib=16304`, `vram_used_mib=748`, `gtt_used_mib=69`.
- `host_mem_available_mib=4254`; active swap: **`/dev/zram0` only**.
- Hypothetical post-4GiB capacity: `12208` MiB; hypothetical free after existing use and pool: `11460` MiB.
- `USERSPACE_EXPERIMENT_ELIGIBLE`, zero reasons; `KERNEL_SWAP_BLOCKED` and `physical_vram_residency_guaranteed=false`.

Five one-second global samples drifted from VRAM 747.820 to 741.305 MiB, GTT 69.07 MiB unchanged. Those were read-only and not proof of reservation.

## Direct GPU-only benchmark (old v080 utility, current v085 checkout)

```sh
nopen-v080-gpu-bench --backend opencl --mode direct --pool-mib 1024 --operations 32 --full-passes 1 --ack I_ACCEPT_1GIB_GPU_ONLY_NO_SWAP
```

Four 256MiB OpenCL segments. Full **1,073,741,824 bytes** tagged, written first, then read/verified. `V080_GPU_FULL_ARENA_VERIFIED`, `V080_GPU_BENCH_PASS`, `full_arena_integrity=YES`, **1.084 seconds**, no kernel swap.

| Chunk | Write (MiB/s) | Read (MiB/s) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 4 KiB | 164.92 | 266.88 |
| 64 KiB | 2000.79 | 2653.69 |
| 1 MiB | 10371.17 | 9883.77 |
| 4 MiB | 9476.80 | 19325.08 |

This is GPU storage throughput, not swap throughput, host-memory savings, frame rate or guaranteed non-evictable VRAM.

## New v085 Holder: 1 GiB for 15 seconds

```sh
nopen-v085-reserved-holder --backend opencl --pool-mib 1024 --seconds 15 --ack I_ACCEPT_1GIB_HOLDER_NO_SWAP
```

Success `V085_HOLDER_FULL_ARENA_PASS`, `V085_HOLDER_FINAL_INTEGRITY_PASS` and `V085_HOLDER_PASS`. Full readback correct before and after hold, one page checked every second. Process execution took about 16.5 seconds.

| Sample | Global VRAM used | Global GTT used | MemAvailable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Before | 763 MiB | 75 MiB | 4005 MiB |
| Hold start | 1957 MiB | 81 MiB | 4049 MiB |
| Hold +5s | 1949 MiB | 75 MiB | 4049 MiB |
| Hold +10s | 1944 MiB | 75 MiB | 4051 MiB |
| Immediately after close | 1931 MiB | 75 MiB | 4057 MiB |

**Open question:** VRAM stays approximately **1,168 MiB** above baseline immediately after store-close. This alone does **not** establish leak, pin, driver defect or attribution to Nopen; global GPU counters may reflect delayed frees/caching/other processes. No delayed post-process time-series has been provided.

## What this does NOT establish

- A kernel/driver partition: **none**.
- The game seeing only 12 GiB: **no**.
- Non-evictable GPU residency: **not proven**.
- Kernel SWAP safety: **not tested**.
- GPU reset data survival and reclaim progress: **not proven**.

Next safe check: read-only VRAM/GTT observer with 12 samples at five-second intervals, no new GPU allocation first.
